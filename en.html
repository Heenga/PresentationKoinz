Koinz Journey

Next steps

From the first pitch to a fully operating board, in six steps.

  1. 01

    Individual Pitch

    In a one-on-one session, the company presents its business thesis and key metrics directly to the Koinz analysis team.

  2. 02

    Koinz Internal Review

    The pitch information goes to an internal committee, which assesses the company’s potential and Koinz’s real ability to add value to the business.

  3. 03

    Tailored Proposal

    Based on the company’s stage, needs and mapped goals, Koinz sends a structured proposal suited to where the company is today.

  4. 04

    Contract Signing

    After review and alignment on terms, the parties formalize the agreement and move on to signing the contract.

  5. 05

    Board Selection and Setup

    The company pitches to interested advisors, who apply for a seat; it then receives the list of candidates and chooses who will join its board.

  6. 06

    Ongoing Support

    Throughout the partnership, the company has hands-on support, access to the Koinz network, strategic connections and continuous mentoring.

Step 05 in detail

Board composition

How your board is formed, in four moves.

  1. 01

    Presentation

    The company is first introduced to Koinz Capital advisors, who can ask their questions.

  2. 02

    Application

    Advisors apply to join the company’s advisory board.

  3. 03

    Matching

    Koinz shares the list of applicants and matches the business’s pain points and goals with the advisors’ experience.

  4. 04

    Decision

    The company chooses the advisors and the Operating Partner who will make up its board.
